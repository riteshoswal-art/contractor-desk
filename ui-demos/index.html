What all three keep from your product rules

  • Bids are grouped by unit. ₹/sq ft, ₹/day and fixed prices are never ranked against each other.
  • A start up to 2 days later is shown but clearly marked, and can't be chosen until confirmed.
  • Ratings show the number of reviews. No reviews means "Not yet rated"; fewer than three is "too few to judge".
  • Phone numbers stay hidden until a contractor is chosen.
  • Contractor screens work in English, हिन्दी and मराठी (Hindi and Marathi still need a native speaker's review).

How to review

  • Use the dark bar at the bottom of each concept to jump between the seven screens.
  • Try it: fill in a request, switch the contractor screens to Hindi, send a bid, and on "Compare & choose" select a bidder, then open the Job tab.
  • Narrow the window (or open on a phone) to check the mobile layout.
  • No statistics are invented: the home page shows product rules, not user counts.
